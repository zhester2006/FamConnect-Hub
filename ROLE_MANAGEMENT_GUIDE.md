# Family Role Management Guide

## Overview
Parents can now assign different roles to family members, providing flexibility in access and permissions.

## Available Roles

### 👑 Parent
- **Full Access**: Complete control over all features
- **Permissions**:
  - Manage family members and their roles
  - Create, approve, and delete chores
  - Approve shopping requests and event submissions
  - Access all dashboards and reports
  - Manage rewards and points
  - View and edit all family data

### 👤 Member
- **Limited Admin**: Adult family member with view access
- **Permissions**:
  - View family calendar and events
  - Participate in family chat and wall
  - View shopping lists
  - See chore schedules
  - **Cannot**: Approve requests, assign chores, manage family members

### 👶 Child
- **Gamified Experience**: Limited access with points & rewards
- **Permissions**:
  - View their own space/dashboard
  - Complete assigned chores (requires approval)
  - Earn points and badges
  - Request items for shopping list
  - Submit reading logs
  - Add events (requires approval)
  - Participate in chat and family wall
  - Redeem rewards from shop

## How to Manage Roles

### From Parent Dashboard
1. Navigate to your **Dashboard**
2. Scroll to **Family Members** section
3. Each member card shows their current role
4. Click on the role button to open role selector
5. Choose new role: Parent, Member, or Child
6. Changes take effect immediately

### From Family Management Page
1. Navigate to **Family** tab in bottom navigation
2. View all family members with detailed info
3. See role statistics at the top (Parents, Members, Children count)
4. Click any member's role to change it
5. Add new members with specific roles using "Add Member" button

## Role Change Rules
- ✅ Parents can change anyone's role except their own
- ❌ Cannot change your own role
- ✅ Multiple parents allowed in a family
- ✅ Role changes are instant and sync across all devices

## Use Cases

### Multi-Generational Families
- **Grandparents**: Assign as "Member" to view family activities without admin duties
- **Aunts/Uncles**: "Member" role for staying connected
- **Teenagers**: Start as "Child", promote to "Member" when more mature

### Blended Families
- **Step-parents**: Can be assigned "Parent" role for full co-parenting
- **Older step-siblings**: "Member" for coordination without full child restrictions

### Shared Households
- **Roommates with kids**: Multiple parents managing their own children
- **Extended family living together**: Flexible role assignment

## Best Practices
1. **Start Conservative**: Begin with "Child" role and promote as needed
2. **Clear Communication**: Discuss role changes with family members
3. **Regular Review**: Revisit roles as children grow and responsibilities change
4. **Balance Authority**: Ensure at least 2 parents for backup access

## Security Notes
- Role changes require parent authentication
- All role changes are logged (future feature)
- Children cannot request role changes
- Members have read-only access to sensitive data
